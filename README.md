##  Overview

GiGWork is a web application built for our **Web Programming** course. It aims to bridge the gap between young people looking for flexible, short-term work (tutoring, event help, pet-sitting, freelance design, errands, digital gigs, etc.) and individuals or small businesses who need quick, reliable help.

Unlike traditional gig platforms aimed at adults, GigLink is designed with **age-appropriate safety, mentorship, and skill-building** in mind — recognizing that our users range from teenagers just starting to earn, to young adults building a freelance career.

---

##  Problem Statement

Many young people want to earn money and gain real-world experience, but:
- Most gig platforms (Upwork, TaskRabbit, etc.) require users to be 18+.
- Younger users lack a safe, structured place to find trustworthy gigs.
- There's no easy way for communities to post small, local jobs to nearby youth.
- Existing platforms don't account for varying legal/parental-consent needs across age groups.

GigLink solves this by offering an age-tiered, safety-first gig marketplace.

---

##  Target Audience

- **14–17 (Junior tier):** Light, supervised gigs — tutoring, pet care, yard work, digital tasks. Requires parental/guardian consent.
- **18–24 (Emerging tier):** Freelance gigs, internships, part-time work, skill-based tasks.
- **25–30 (Pro tier):** Higher-value freelance/professional gigs, mentorship opportunities.

---

## ✨ Key Features

-  **User Authentication** — Signup/login with age-based account tiers.
-  **Parental Consent Flow** — Guardian approval required for users under 18.
-  **Gig Listings** — Post, browse, filter, and search gigs by category, location, and pay.
-  **Ratings & Reviews** — Build trust through verified feedback after each gig.
-  **In-app Messaging** — Communicate about gig details without sharing personal contact info upfront.
-  **Skill Badges** — Earn verified badges (e.g., "Reliable," "Great with Kids," "Fast Learner").
-  **Location-based Matching** — Find nearby gigs using geolocation.
-  **Responsive Design** — Fully functional across desktop and mobile.
